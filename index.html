Bio

Bio

I am a Principal Research Scientist at NVIDIA Spatial Intelligence Lab, working on generative world models and simulation research, based in Santa Clara, California. Before joining NVIDIA, I was an AI research scientist at Meta. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Harvard University and my B.A.Sc. from the University of Toronto.
